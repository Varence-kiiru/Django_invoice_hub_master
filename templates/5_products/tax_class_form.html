Tax Classes Overview

Tax classes define the default VAT treatment for products in your catalog:

  • Standard VAT: Products subject to standard VAT rate
  • Zero-Rated: Products with 0% VAT (e.g., exports, basic foods)
  • Exempt: Products exempt from VAT

Each tax class can have multiple VAT rules that define specific conditions for tax application.
