📌 Payment Recording Tips
  • Partial Payments: You can record partial payments. The invoice balance will update automatically.
  • Transaction Reference: Always include reference numbers for reconciliation (M-Pesa code, check #, bank ref, etc.)
  • Payment Date: Use the actual date the payment was received, not today's date if it's backdated.
  • Multiple Payments: One invoice can have multiple payment records. Each will be added to the total.
