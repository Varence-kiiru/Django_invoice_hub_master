💡 Reconciliation Tips
🔍 Match by Reference

Use transaction references (M-Pesa codes, cheque numbers, bank references) to match payments to invoices.

💰 Match by Amount

Look for payments that match invoice totals exactly, or partial payments that add up to the total.

📅 Match by Date

Consider the timing - payments are usually made within a few days of invoice issuance.

⚠️ Common Issues
  • Partial payments not recorded
  • Multiple invoices paid with one transaction
  • Incorrect transaction references
  • Bank fees reducing payment amount
✅ Best Practices
  • Reconcile payments daily
  • Keep bank statements organized
  • Verify client information
  • Document reconciliation decisions
