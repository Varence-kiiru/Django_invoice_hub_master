About VAT Rules

VAT Rules define conditional applicability of tax rates based on product tax classifications. Each rule is applied in priority order to determine the appropriate tax rate for calculation.

Priority: Higher priority rules are evaluated first. Common scenarios: Zero-rated exports (P100), Exempt supplies (P90), Standard rate (P10).
